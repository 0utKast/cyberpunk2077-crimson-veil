Crimson Veil / Velo Carmesí (v0.9.0 — Prologue + Polished Act I + Complete Act II: Liturgy of Ash)
Author: 0utKast
Compatibility: Cyberpunk 2077 2.x — 100% Base Game Compatible (No Phantom Liberty / Zero ep1 dependencies)
Required Core Frameworks: RED4ext, ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware, redscript, Audioware, Cyber Engine Tweaks

OVERVIEW:
Crimson Veil is a standalone techno-occult narrative quest chain for Cyberpunk 2077 starring Misty Olszewski (strictly non-combatant) and V investigating Night City's nocturnal biosculpt network "The Pale Chorus" and experimental compound NOX-9.

NEW IN v0.9.0 (COMPLETE ACT II "LITURGY OF ASH" + ACT I FINAL POLISH):
- Complete Act II campaign arc triggered after Act I debrief: Misty's holocall + recap SMS lead V to the ambushed **Biotechnica Flats** convoy (3 forensic clues uncovering Mateo M-09's sacrifice and Lucía's transfer), the **closed Badlands geothermal bunker** (outer sentries + 365 nm UV emitter), meeting **Lucía Reyes** (Subject L-14), and confronting **Dra. Helena Solís ("The Cantor")** inside the **NOX-9B Bioreactor Chamber**.
- Two new custom-rigged NPCs with locked appearances, dedicated workspots, 30 FPS JALI lip-sync, and 100% original ElevenLabs synthetic voices (Full English & Spanish — 410 studio voice lines + 4 custom SFX): **Lucía Reyes** (`cv_lucia`) and **Dra. Helena Solís** (`cv_solis`).
- Three playable endings (**Ending A · The Second Serum**, **Ending B · Pact in the Penumbra**, **Ending C · Dawn of Blood**) with a full rooftop epilogue alongside Misty and rescued Lucía granting **+4,000 E$, +1,500 XP**, iconic Tech revolver **"Alba Carmesí"**, and an expanded **10-shard** Codex.
- Final Act I polish: relocated Dr. Adrián Valdés into a secluded street-level southern Little China back alley fitting his fugitive status (direct GPS access), shifted the West Pier cryo terminal cleanly into the open courtyard center clear of ambient workspots, and unified all dialogue hubs so **blue informational questions appear on top** and **yellow progression choices appear below**.

LANGUAGE & VOICE TRANSPARENCY NOTE:
- Full native bilingual voice acting and localization in both English (205 lines) and Spanish (205 lines) plus 4 custom SFX (414 audio files total) are included in v0.9.0 and switch automatically with Cyberpunk 2077's language settings.
- To strictly respect copyright and the original game's voice actors, NO CDPR audio is extracted or cloned. Misty and all new characters are voiced using 100% original synthetic voices created from scratch via ElevenLabs Voice Design.

HOW TO START:
- Automatically starts via an incoming holocall from Misty once Act 2 is reached (after completing Playing for Time) and V is at least Level 10 (out of combat, away from Misty's Esoterica).
- Can also be started manually at any time via the optional Cyber Engine Tweaks (CET) debug panel.

INSTALLATION:
1. Install required open-source frameworks separately (RED4ext, ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware, redscript, Audioware, and Cyber Engine Tweaks — never bundled in this archive).
2. Extract this ZIP directly into your Cyberpunk 2077 root directory.
